## Rights and complaints routine

- Record the received date, contact, nature of the request and applicable deadline. A privacy request may arrive through either published channel; do not require email as the only route.
- Acknowledge promptly, clarify only necessary questions, and verify identity proportionately. Do not ask for a national ID number or identity document by default. Use an agreed private method if sensitive verification is truly necessary.
- For applicable EU/UK GDPR requests, act without undue delay and normally within one month. Explain a legally permitted extension within the original month. Handle any UK-specific clarification pause only where its conditions apply; do not automatically apply it to EU requests.
- For applicable Moroccan Law 09-08 requests, Article 7 access is without delay and Article 8 necessary correction/erasure/blocking of nonconforming data is subject to ten clear days. Any shorter applicable deadline prevails. Do not use a universal one-month delay for every jurisdiction.
- Where UK complaints duties apply, acknowledge within 30 days; begin investigation without undue delay, provide progress information as needed, communicate the outcome and actions taken. The acknowledgement period is not the rights-request deadline.
- Search only records under the maintainer's control. Explain when a request concerns GitHub/Google/Discord/Figma/Roblox's independent processing and point to that platform. Do not claim authority to delete its security logs or a user's published assets.
- Handle objections to legitimate-interest support processing separately. Stop unless a lawful overriding reason applies; document a decision and explain it.
- Keep the minimal outcome record under the retention schedule. Give relevant authority contact details. Do not require withdrawal of a regulator complaint or other legal right.

## Legitimate-interest assessment to adopt

Proposed purpose: reply to a user-initiated question, investigate an identified bug and maintain a reliable free tool. Necessity: use the minimum correspondence/version/warning information; ask for a reduced example only if needed. Balance: support is optional, users expect a reply, no advertising/resale/profiling, private examples are not published, short attachment retention, accessible objection/deletion channels. Younger users need additional care and clear explanations. Record the assessment against actual recipients, content sensitivity, age/audience and safeguards before adopting it. This is not a blanket basis for all future processing.

## Primary guidance checked on 5 October 2026

- [ICO privacy information](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/individual-rights/the-right-to-be-informed/what-privacy-information-should-we-provide/): identity, purposes, lawful bases, recipients, transfers, retention, rights and complaint route.
- [EDPB territorial guidance](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/sites/default/files/files/file1/edpb_guidelines_3_2018_territorial_scope_after_public_consultation_en_1.pdf): free offerings can be in scope; global accessibility alone is insufficient.
- [ICO legitimate interests](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/lawful-basis/legitimate-interests/what-is-the-legitimate-interests-basis/): purpose, necessity and balancing assessment.
- [ICO current request guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/subject-access-requests/a-guide-to-subject-access/) and [EDPB rights guidance](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/sme/be-compliant/respect-individuals-rights_en): conditions and response handling; do not promise universal portability.
- [ICO complaints guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/how-to-deal-with-data-protection-complaints/what-do-we-do-when-we-receive-a-complaint/): 30-day acknowledgement and prompt investigation/outcome. The UK complaints duty took effect on 23 June 2026.
- [CNDP Law 09-08](https://www.cndp.ma/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Loi-09-08-Fr.pdf), [notification process](https://www.cndp.ma/notifier-un-traitement/) and [website guide](https://www.cndp.ma/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/CNDP-guide-conformite-sites-web-fr.pdf): Moroccan controller identity, formalities, transfer assessment and rights.
- [CMA fair terms guidance, updated July 2026](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/writing-a-fair-contract-for-customers): clarity, fairness, mandatory rights and balanced changes, including relevant free digital contracts/notices.
- [EU Digital Content Directive](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2019/770/oj): qualified scope, changes and mandatory protections; do not assume all free tools automatically fall inside or outside it.
