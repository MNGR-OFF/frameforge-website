The detailed owner brief already fixes the audience, mechanism, colors, logo, pages, and actions. The owner expressly delegates practical design choices. This session continues code-first work already underway, rather than imposing a new image-comp approval round or aesthetic interview.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Editable structure is the story. Show a designed launch panel becoming an Explorer hierarchy immediately. A quiet steel workbench carries the mechanism; decoration never substitutes for it.

OWN-WORLD: Obsidian ground, graphite surfaces, precise steel rules, concentrated ember actions. Manrope’s open geometric forms carry the display; plain sans text serves reading. Monospace is reserved for hierarchy/code/measurements. Border-defined panels, restrained radii, no decorative kickers.

STORY: Visitors understand the native conversion, its boundaries, and the seven steps to Studio. They leave through honest installation guidance and can test a masked hover concept.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Large three-line headline left, two plugin installation actions below. Right, the source interface, forging bridge, and native hierarchy form one specific conversion exhibit. Preserve generous open space around it. Mobile stacks the headline, actions, and exhibit.

FORM: A conversion workbench expanded from the owner’s explicit digital-forge brief. No concept tournament: practical decisions were explicitly delegated. Established code is refined with Impeccable’s craft floor.

SIGNATURE: A short forging cadence connects the design and highlighted native layer; it pauses offscreen, with tab visibility, or by user choice. The live masked stripe button lets people change movement and 2D tilt, with a static reduced-motion equivalent.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
